[h1]Japan - The Kitayama Concord[/h1]
[b]Version 0.3.0-alpha.78 - Unlisted external testing build for EU5 1.3[/b]

[i]What if Japan avoided the Sengoku Jidai by turning its warrior houses toward commerce, infrastructure, and constitutional compromise?[/i]

After resolving the Nanboku-cho conflict and moving toward Plutocracy, the leader of the Japanese Shogunate may proclaim the [b]Kitayama Concord[/b]. The old clans become Chartered Buke Houses: commercial warrior lineages that compete through credit, buildings, votes, and economic influence rather than private war.

[h2]How to form the Concord[/h2]
[list=1]
[*]Lead the Japanese Shogunate.
[*]Resolve the Nanboku-cho situation.
[*]Be at peace with at least 25 Stability (15 for AI-controlled Japan).
[*]Move Aristocracy vs Plutocracy to 0 or farther toward Plutocracy.
[*]Open the Japanese Shogunate, select Members, and use [b]Proclaim the Kitayama Concord[/b].
[*]Open the new Concord through its native bottom-screen button and use [b]Ratify the Kitayama Concord[/b].
[/list]

[h2]Alpha testing[/h2]
This is a feature-rich but unfinished external alpha. Long campaigns and ordinary play are particularly valuable. Please report unclear requirements, balance problems, AI failures, repeated events, election or law problems, Treasury exploits, building issues, Ezo behavior, performance pauses, missing localization, and save/reload failures.

When reporting a problem, include the EU5 and mod versions, country and date, Concord leader, Cohesion and Entrenchment, reproduction steps, other enabled mods, and a save, screenshot, or log excerpt when available.

[h2]AI Japan and House campaigns[/h2]
AI Japan can use Concord treasury programs, complete eligible crisis programs, authorize national projects, and order limited physical infrastructure with normal costs. When a human plays a Japanese clan or Chartered House, AI Japan follows House-oriented constitutional choices and the later Concord moves toward House Privilege. This temporary release rule persists after leadership changes. Human event choices and ballots remain under player control. Long-campaign AI testing is still pending.

[h2]Compatibility and known issues[/h2]
[b]Compatibility warning:[/b] This mod replaces the vanilla Middle Kingdom membership rules to keep subordinate Concord Houses from joining it independently. Without this restriction, their tributary obligations can interfere with Japan's wars. Japan retains its own access to Middle Kingdom diplomacy. Keep this restriction enabled; mods that replace the Middle Kingdom require a compatibility patch. The Concord also replaces the shared law-voting resolution, a shared law-initiation trigger, Japanese clan-building definitions, and country-rank naming. These overlaps can affect compatibility even when another mod adds no Japanese content. Recheck compatibility after an EU5 update.

[h2]Northern colonization[/h2]
AI Japan pursues Ezo settlement after Concord formation and upper Manchuria/Sakhalin after completing the Hundred-Year Concord. Dedicated paid charter actions work under the western-only AI colonization rule. The AI receives basic colonization technology and northern charts at the relevant stage; native range, charter costs and settlement progress still apply. Players have no Entrenchment restriction on colonization. The Northern Colonial Administration can be chartered from the first northern holding after Concord formation.

Compatibility: this version also replaces the native create_colonial_charter and abandon_colonial_charter action definitions to route Concord AI northern settlement through the dedicated actions and retain its active northern charters. Other countries and human controls retain vanilla behavior. Mods replacing those actions require a compatibility patch.
